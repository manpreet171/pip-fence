# TRANSFER TEST — the paper instrument for Kuzhi (written before any child sees it)

Within-child, pre → post → 48 h. Same eight items each time, orders below. Paper and a pencil; no
device. The adult reads each item aloud once, verbatim, repeats on request, says nothing else,
60 seconds per item, scores after the child has left the room. Raw records go in `PILOT-RESULTS.md`.

## The ring sheet
Each item is printed as a ring of fourteen dots with one dot filled black (the start). The child
is told how many steps to count on, anticlockwise (an arrow shows the direction), and marks the
dot she lands on. The start dot itself is never counted — the instruction says "the first step is
the next dot." Trained ring size is 14; trained step sizes are 2, 3, 4, 6.

## Items
| # | Kind | Read aloud exactly | Answer (dots after start) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | trained | "Start on the black dot. Count on five. Mark where you land." | 5 |
| 2 | trained | "Start on the black dot. Count on seven. Mark where you land." | 7 |
| 3 | **control** | "Here is a number line from nought to twenty. Start at eight and jump back three. Mark where you land." | 5 (backwards on a line, never taught) |
| 4 | trained | "Start on the black dot. Count on nine. Mark where you land." | 9 |
| 5 | trained | "Start on the dot just before the corner. Count on four. Mark where you land." | crosses the corner; 4 |
| 6 | **control** | "Twelve stones in a bag. Take out four. How many are left?" | 8 (subtraction, never taught) |
| 7 | trained | "Start on the black dot. Count on eleven. Mark where you land." | 11 |
| 8 | trained | "Start on the black dot. Count on sixteen. Mark where you land." | 2 (past a full lap) |
| 9 | trained (ladder) | "This cup has five stones. One more goes in. How many now?" | 6 |
| 10 | trained (ladder) | "This cup has seven stones. Is that even or odd?" | odd |

Items 9 and 10 are scored only for a child who reached the count or even levels; they are marked
"not reached" otherwise and never counted against her.

Trained items use step sizes **not** in the game's set (5, 7, 9, 11, 16 and a corner crossing) on
the game's ring size, so they are near transfer without repeating a trained move. Items 3 and 6 are
pre-registered controls scored /2 separately.

## Registered prediction (do not edit after the first child)
Trained items may rise pre → post; the control items should not. If the controls rise as much as
the trained items, the effect is not the mechanic. If trained scores do not rise pre → post in at
least 2 of 3 children, the video says, in these words: *call-the-pit showed no measurable
near-transfer signal in this pilot.*

Also recorded, from the game's own log: **call accuracy over the session**, per child, as a
sequence — the learning curve the mechanic produces on every move.

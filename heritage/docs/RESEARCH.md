# RESEARCH — what the three passes found, in one page

Three research passes ran on 15 Sep 2026, each with sources and confidence marks. Read them for the
evidence; this page is the synthesis that led to `CONCEPT.md`.

| Memo | Scope | What it settled |
|---|---|---|
| `RESEARCH-INDIA.md` | Śulba Sūtras, Līlāvatī, sowing games of Tamil Nadu / Karnataka / Andhra, Chaupar and Gyān Caupaṛ, Bhūtasaṃkhyā and Kaṭapayādi, NCERT use today | The sowing rule *family* is well attested; no single seed count is. | `RESEARCH-WORLD.md` | Rithmomachia, the mancala family, Ur / Senet / Hounds and Jackals, Patolli and the logic games, counting devices, puzzle texts | Only Rithmomachia and Ur have surviving rulebooks. No trial shows mancala improves arithmetic; a linear numbered race board does (Siegler & Ramani). Alcuin's puzzles were written for the young. |
| `RESEARCH-AI.md` | Where a model earns its place in each candidate, what a Nerdy panel respects, what is theatre, feasibility against the architecture proven in Rung | Only mechanics that produce a hand-made action log can use the proven pipeline. Verse riddles and number ciphers put the numbers in the prompt. The sowing game needs one added step — the call — to produce the log. |

## Convergence
All three ranked the sowing game with a pre-move landing call first or second. The India pass
preferred it for age fit and low cultural load; the world pass for attested, living rules; the AI
pass because it is the one candidate that is both a different product from Rung and structurally
able to reuse Rung's safety and measurement layers.

## What we do not claim
- That any sowing game has been shown to improve arithmetic. Nobody has run that trial.
- That our rules are "the" Pallanguzhi. They are one simplified version, and the call is ours.
- That anything here is "Vedic maths", 5,000 years old, or invented in one place.

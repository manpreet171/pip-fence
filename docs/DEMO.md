# DEMO — the 3-minute shot list

Product: **Rung — build your village.** Adaptive maths where **the maths IS the building
mechanic**, in a generated world that remembers the child.
Rule: if a feature isn't in a shot below, it's backlog or bin.

| Time | Shot | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Cold open on an empty village. "Most maths apps bolt a game on top of a worksheet. Here the maths *is* the game." | The thesis |
| 0:15–0:45 | Two build choices appear (🌉 bridge / 🏠 cottage), each with its own cost. Child **picks one** — their village, their decision. Work out the cost → it gets built, tile pops in. | Math is the mechanic + autonomy |
| 0:45–1:15 | The village **visibly grows** — their actual building is drawn into the scene (chimney smoke, turning wheel, glowing lanterns), and the sky moves toward dusk as it fills. The AI narrates it, **referencing something built earlier**. Reload — the village is still there and the buddy greets them by name. | A world that shows their real choices + memory |
| 1:05–1:30 | Type a wrong cost. Buddy diagnoses the exact slip and asks a guiding question — never gives the number. Point at the footer: every cost generated and checked in code. | Never-wrong help, correct by construction |
| 1:30–1:50 | A few correct in a row — difficulty ladder visibly climbs. | Adaptive to this child |
| 1:50–2:25 | Cut to `/measure`. Two children diverge. The honest number: **wastes 24% of questions vs 58%** for a fixed curriculum; frustration **2% vs 27%** — and it holds **even when the engine's assumptions are wrong**. | Measured, non-circular |
| 2:25–2:45 | Read the scope line: simulated children, not yet a classroom. "I'm showing what I can prove." | Intellectual honesty |
| 2:45–3:00 | "Code owns the truth. AI owns the world. The engine owns the difficulty." | The architecture in one line |

**Numbers (deterministic, from `/measure` + `evals/engine_eval2.mjs`):** wasted questions
24% vs 58%; frustration 2% vs 27%; 600 simulated children.

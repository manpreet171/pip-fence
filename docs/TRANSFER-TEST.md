# TRANSFER TEST — the paper instrument (written before any child sees it)

Authority: CONCEPT-V3.2 §5. Three children, no arms, within-child. Administered three times per child:
**pre** (immediately before the 20-minute session), **post** (immediately after), **48 h** retest.
Same eight items each time, order reshuffled per the table below. Paper and pencil, no device.

## Rules for the adult (read before each administration)
1. Read each item aloud **verbatim, once**. Repeat verbatim on request. Nothing else is said.
2. **No hints, no feedback, no "good", no "try again".** A neutral "thank you" after each answer.
3. 60 seconds per item. If no answer at 60 s, write `—` and move on.
4. Score after the child has left the room. Trained items /6, control items /2, **separately**.
5. Record the administration in `docs/PILOT-RESULTS.md` raw: child code, date/time, order used, answers.

## Items
Trained shapes are 2×3, 3×3, 3×4, 4×3, 4×5, 2×5. No item repeats a trained shape in either order;
no two items are commutes of each other.

| # | Kind | Item (read aloud exactly) | Answer |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | trained | "Three times six. What is three times six?" | 18 |
| 2 | trained | "Five times three. What is five times three?" | 15 |
| 3 | **control** | "Something times four makes eight. What is the missing number?" | 2 |
| 4 | trained | "Four times six. What is four times six?" | 24 |
| 5 | trained | "There are seven plates. Each plate has three buns. How many buns altogether?" | 21 |
| 6 | **control** | "Eighteen pencils go into boxes. Each box holds six pencils. How many boxes?" | 3 |
| 7 | trained | "A bike has two wheels. How many wheels on nine bikes?" | 18 |
| 8 | trained | "Six children each have five stickers. How many stickers altogether?" | 30 |

Written form for the child's sheet: items 1, 2, 4 as `3 × 6 = □`, `5 × 3 = □`, `4 × 6 = □`; item 3 as
`□ × 4 = 8`; items 5–8 as the sentence.

## Orders (fixed in advance, one per administration)
- Pre: 1 5 3 7 2 8 6 4
- Post: 4 2 6 8 1 3 5 7
- 48 h: 7 3 1 6 4 5 8 2

## Registered prediction (do not edit after the first child)
Trained items may rise pre → post. **Control items should not.** If control items rise as much as trained
items, the effect is not the mechanic. If trained scores do not rise pre → post in at least 2 of 3
children, the video says, in these words: *build-is-the-problem showed no measurable near-transfer
signal in this pilot.*

## Disclosures said on camera
- "The comparison build is my own earlier build, which is a conflict of interest."
- "I administered the post-test myself, unblinded — I knew exactly what each child had just done."
- "n=3. No p-value. Three case studies, and a null if that is what we got."
